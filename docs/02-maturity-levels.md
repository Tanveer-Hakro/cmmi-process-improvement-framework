# The Five CMMI Maturity Levels

## Level 1 — Initial

Processes are described as unpredictable and chaotic. Success depends heavily on individual
effort, formal process management is limited, and work tends to be reactive.

**Characteristics**
- inconsistent outcomes,
- limited process documentation,
- high risk due to undefined processes.

## Level 2 — Managed

Basic project-management practices are established. Projects follow documented procedures, and
management gains better visibility into project status.

**Characteristics**
- documented project-management processes,
- cost and schedule tracking,
- requirements management and stakeholder involvement.

## Level 3 — Defined

Processes are standardized across the organization. The emphasis shifts toward proactive and
strategic process management.

**Characteristics**
- standardized processes across projects,
- tailored procedures for organizational needs,
- proactive risk management.

## Level 4 — Quantitatively Managed

Organizations use statistical and quantitative methods to monitor and manage processes.

**Characteristics**
- statistical process control,
- data-driven decision-making,
- predictive models for future performance.

## Level 5 — Optimizing

Organizations focus on continuous process improvement using innovation and systematic analysis.

**Characteristics**
- continuous improvement,
- systematic use of data,
- proactive identification and prevention of inefficiencies.
