# CMM vs. CMMI

The source contrasts CMM and CMMI primarily in terms of scope.

- **CMM** is described as initially focused on software development.
- **CMMI** is described as integrating multiple disciplines, including systems engineering,
  service delivery, and product development.
- The source also notes staged and continuous improvement paths within CMMI.

The main conceptual difference is therefore the move from a more narrowly focused maturity model
toward a broader integrated framework for organizational process improvement.
